How to build new numbers

Combine hundreds, tens, and ones. 이백 = 200; 삼백 = 300. Omit empty places: 407 = 사백칠. Use 십 and 백, rather than 일십 and 일백.

Phone numbers are read digit by digit. 공 = zero in phone numbers; 영 = zero in general number reading.
